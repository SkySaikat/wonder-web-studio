# Wonderweb Studio: U.S. buyer-intent SEO plan

Prepared 25 November 2026. The first implementation is local only. The owner controls production publication.

## The business objective

Build a repeatable source of qualified U.S. inquiries for website projects and ongoing marketing. Favor business owners comparing agencies, website scope, redesigns and specialist marketing help. Treat general traffic, image impressions and AI mentions as supporting measures rather than the result we sell.

Start with established home-service businesses and contractors: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodeling and related trades. A suitable inquiry has an actual business, an accepted service market, a specific project or marketing problem, access to a decision-maker and a plausible budget for the published offer. Do not optimize this program around students, design jobs, free templates, consumers looking for a plumber, or people seeking a DIY website tutorial.

## SEO, AEO and image rules

1. **Useful content before volume.** Every new URL must answer a distinct buying need with enough information to be useful on its own. Do not publish a city-name substitution matrix. Google identifies doorway and scaled-content abuse in its [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
2. **Images explain something.** Use authorized project images, relevant diagrams and properly labeled concepts. Set dimensions, responsive delivery and meaningful alternatives. Image count alone is not a quality target. See [Google’s image guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images).
3. **Image entries in sitemaps are valid.** Keep the HTML page as the main URL and nest its image references. Google permits images in the existing sitemap or a separate image sitemap. Do not turn every image into a standalone HTML-page entry. [Image sitemap documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/image-sitemaps).
4. **Schema describes reality.** Use Organization plus Service/areaServed for a remote agency; do not fabricate a LocalBusiness address in every city. Structured data must match visible facts. FAQ markup does not promise a Google FAQ rich result.
5. **Markdown is an alternate representation.** Keep /content/...md aligned with the HTML and link back to the canonical page. Keep llms.txt and content discovery coherent, but do not promise a ranking boost from those files. Google says no extra AI-specific files or special schema are needed for AI Overviews or AI Mode. [AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
6. **Answer buyer questions directly.** Start each relevant section with a clear answer, then scope, examples and limitations. Include attributable project evidence, current pricing context, stable entity details and primary references where they help. AEO is not a different set of facts hidden in metadata.
7. **Indexability is not indexing.** A successful HTTP response, canonical, crawl permission and sitemap inclusion are prerequisites we can verify. Google decides whether and when to index. Submit the production sitemap and inspect priority URLs after the owner deploys. Do not submit localhost or promise immediate ranking.

## How to choose the next keywords using the connected data

1. Export 28-day and 90-day U.S. Search Console queries and pages separately. Exclude brand queries when evaluating new-customer discovery. Do not add query rows to page totals or treat impressions as monthly search volume.
2. Identify relevant queries whose selected page is useful but underdeveloped. Positions 4–20 can be a starting filter, not a guarantee of quick improvement. Check that the query has agency/service-buying intent.
3. Use OpenSEO metrics when available to add volume, KD, CPC and intent to the candidate CSV. Preserve missing values. Do not use paid-ad competition as organic KD or sum grouped close-variant volumes.
4. Inspect mobile and desktop results in the intended U.S. market. Record competing page types, local-pack prominence, authority, evidence and offer. A nonlocal SERP or one search sample is incomplete evidence.
5. Score buyer fit first, then existing visibility, evidence quality, SERP suitability and production effort. Avoid an invented numerical opportunity score while core inputs are unavailable.
6. Assign one primary URL. Improve an existing page if it already owns that intent. Create a new page only when it serves a distinct decision and has a clear place in the site hierarchy.

## Measurement and sales feedback

Track each important landing page through: U.S. non-brand impressions → relevant organic visits → accepted inquiry → qualified opportunity → booked conversation → proposal → won client → revenue. Keep raw form events, phone clicks and free-guide downloads separate. A free-guide lead is useful when it fits the service and receives the agreed follow-up; it is not automatically a qualified sales opportunity.

Use a weekly sheet with page URL, service, market, source/medium, query group where available, accepted inquiries, qualified inquiries, booked calls, proposals and wins. Use aggregate Search Console data for search analysis; do not claim to identify the individual query behind a person’s visit. Do not send names, email addresses or other form details to web-analytics event parameters.

Check form success/error behavior and the booking CTA without sending unsolicited test inquiries to real customers. The current booking interface requests a time and says confirmation follows the studio’s reply; measure it as a meeting request, not an automatically confirmed appointment. An external calendar integration remains a separate implementation decision.

A planning example, not a forecast: to win three clients at a 20% qualified-opportunity close rate requires 15 qualified opportunities. At a hypothetical 3% qualified-inquiry rate from relevant visits, that requires 500 relevant visits. Replace both assumptions with Wonderweb’s actual data; this example does not predict what 90 days of SEO will deliver. Do not rely on a new organic-content program as the only channel for near-term client acquisition.
