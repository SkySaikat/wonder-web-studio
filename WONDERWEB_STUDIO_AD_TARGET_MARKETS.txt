WONDERWEB STUDIO — AD TARGET MARKET MASTER LIST
Research date: 11 November 2026
Goal: US$50,000 monthly agency revenue (not profit).
Initial agency acquisition ad budget: US$3,000–$7,500/month.

This is a strategic shortlist, not a measured ranking of advertising profitability. No city-level paid CPC, lead-to-client rate or acquisition cost has been obtained from your ad account. Population and business data indicate possible market fit; they do not establish buying intent or profitable client acquisition. The existing Search Console evidence is organic, not paid conversion data.

Entries are city/metro planning groups. Some are counties or multi-city regions. Verify exact selectable geographic targets and boundaries in each ad platform; do not import this document as a ready-made location upload. Avoid overlapping city, metro and radius campaigns.

SPECIALIST TESTS: United Arab Emirates, Singapore, with locally appropriate offers and language support where required.

FIRST LAUNCH
At US$3,000/month: one US campaign around Dallas–Fort Worth and Tampa, narrowing to one if keyword forecasts show the budget is insufficient. Keep the same service offer across the comparison. Do not spread this budget over the full list.
At US$5,000/month: indicative allocation US$3,000 US Search, US$1,500 Brisbane Search, US$500 retargeting (only if the audience is large enough; otherwise keep in Search).
At US$7,500/month: indicative allocation US$4,000 US Search, US$1,750 Brisbane Search, US$1,000 Calgary test, US$750 retargeting. Add Calgary only if keyword forecasts and initial lead quality support a useful test; otherwise retain that budget in proven markets.
These are test allocations, not performance or client-count forecasts. Change them based on qualified opportunities, collected revenue and acquisition economics.

BEST INITIAL BUSINESS FIT
Established roofing, HVAC/air conditioning, plumbing, electrical, remodeling/renovation, restoration and other home-service businesses. Qualify for an operating business, decision-maker access, delivery capacity, a marketing problem your offer solves, ability to pay your service fee and a separate client media budget where needed. US home-service terminology should be localized to Australian/Canadian/UK usage.
For broader small-business expansion, test a separate offer for B2B professional services and established multi-location businesses. Do not mix every industry or low-price one-off services into the same campaign.

REVENUE MODEL — CURRENT LIST PRICES
13 Full Studio clients x US$3,999 = US$51,987/month.
26 Growth clients x US$1,999 = US$51,974/month.
Mixed example: 10 Full Studio x US$3,999 + 6 Growth x US$1,999 = US$51,984/month.
This is agency service revenue before expenses, discounts and churn. Client advertising spend is separate. One-time website project revenue is not monthly recurring revenue. Recurring revenue at a point in time is not the same as cash collected during that month. Plan delivery capacity as the client roster grows.
At an illustrative 20% close rate from attended qualified sales calls, 13 wins would require 65 such calls, before allowing for churn. This is an assumption, not your measured close rate or a first-month promise.

GEOGRAPHY AND CAMPAIGN NOTES
- Greater Toronto examples: Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill and Oakville. Greater Vancouver examples: Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, Coquitlam and Langley. These sit within the broader market plan, not extra non-overlapping campaigns.
- Dallas–Fort Worth examples: Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco and McKinney. Houston examples: Houston, Katy, Sugar Land and The Woodlands. Phoenix examples: Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler and Gilbert. Use business-location coverage, not household wealth alone: the buyer is the business owner.
- Keep country budgets and results separate. Localize spelling, currency presentation, meeting times, examples and offer wording.
- For Google Search, consider Presence targeting (people in or regularly in selected locations) for this country-focused acquisition test. Geography signals are imperfect; review actual lead business locations.
- Match keywords to agency intent, such as roofing marketing agency, HVAC SEO agency or plumber website design. Homeowner queries such as roof repair near me attract the wrong buyer for Wonderweb Studio.
- Measure qualified attended calls, proposals, signed clients, retained agency fees and collected revenue. A cheap form submission is not necessarily a valuable lead.
- Determine cost-per-qualified-call limits from actual margins, close rates and acceptable acquisition payback. Do not use invented universal CPC or cost-per-lead claims.
- Keep the agency accurately positioned as remote and worldwide. Advertising to a city does not require claiming an office there.
- French-led markets and other language-dependent expansion should wait until the sales and delivery experience can support those languages.
