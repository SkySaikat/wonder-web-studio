## Release checklist

1. Read the source and primary documentation; preserve useful theory without lightly paraphrasing paragraphs.
2. Match one clear search intent. Add useful examples, decisions, exceptions and subject-matter detail. Word targets are editorial planning ranges, not ranking requirements.
3. Verify business facts, platform claims, prices, external links and any measurable results. Do not transfer BuildRight testimonials or achievements to Wonderweb.
4. Link to the relevant service, current pricing and two or three genuinely related guides. Link back from the service or relevant hub.
5. Add topic-specific FAQs, descriptive image text, unique metadata and correct visible-content schema. Remove em dashes.
6. Review on mobile, check all links and run the local content/SEO checks. Resolve overlapping topics before publication.
7. Mark the row approved, deploy through the agreed release process and record the actual date. If unfinished, carry it forward rather than publishing low-quality filler to meet a quota.
