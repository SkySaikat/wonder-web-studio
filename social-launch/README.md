# Wonderweb Studio launch pack

Ready-to-post files:

- `01-new-website.jpg` — 1080 × 1350, portrait 4:5 launch announcement.
- `02-your-digital-team.jpg` — 1080 × 1350, portrait 4:5 service introduction.
- `03-website-launch-reel.mp4` — 1080 × 1920, vertical 9:16, 20 seconds, 30 fps; H.264 video with AAC audio and fast-start playback.
- `video-cover.jpg` — 1080 × 1920 cover for the vertical video.
- `CAPTIONS.md` — copy for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and Pinterest, plus image alt text.

The two images are feed/Pin creatives; the vertical video is the Reel/Short creative. Review each platform’s final crop and cover preview when uploading. All important video copy is visible without audio. The music is an original synthesized instrumental created for this video, with no third-party song or stock recording.

Artwork and text were prepared for the requested website-launch announcement. Nothing has been posted to social accounts, and this task does not deploy any website changes.

Creation: the two campaign posters were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and exported as high-quality JPGs. The video was composed locally from the studio’s original artwork, exact logo paths and Figtree/Instrument Serif typography, with custom animation and original audio. The reproducible render script is in `sources/render_video.py` (not required for posting).
